# Verify continuously, quarantine local damage, and fence ambiguous integrity

Release 1 startup verifies catalog generations, manifests, key bindings, ownership, and active tails through authenticated Durability Frontiers rather than rescanning all sealed data. An incomplete frame strictly after a frontier may be truncated; damage at or before it fences the instance. A bounded Integrity Scrub eventually authenticates every reachable Encrypted Frame and validates segment indexes, metadata, and references, while `positron verify` provides online snapshot and exclusive offline modes with complete machine-readable Verification Reports. An isolated corrupt immutable object becomes a Quarantined Segment: its evidence and affected tenant, signal, and time range remain visible, corrupt records are never returned, and intersecting queries terminate explicitly incomplete. Unaffected traffic may continue only while kernel identity and durability remain trustworthy. Catalog, key, ownership, or acknowledged-frontier ambiguity fences the instance. Release 1 repairs by verified restore into fresh storage, never by fabricating bytes; irreversible Segment Abandonment requires explicit data-loss confirmation and durable governance evidence. Future replicas may repair only from a peer object whose complete authenticated identity matches.
