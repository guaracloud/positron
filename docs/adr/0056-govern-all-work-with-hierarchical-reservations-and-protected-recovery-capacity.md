# Govern all work with hierarchical reservations and protected recovery capacity

Release 1 places ingestion, queries, tails, compaction, retention, backup, restore, encryption, and administration under one Storage Kernel Resource Governor. Global ceilings honor actual process, cgroup, filesystem, and descriptor capacity; tenant quotas nest beneath them. Work reserves bounded memory, queue capacity, and disk headroom before admission, and already-admitted durability work retains enough capacity to finish. Scheduling prioritizes durability and recovery, security and lifecycle administration, ingestion, interactive reads, then ordinary maintenance and backups, with weighted fairness and progress guarantees preventing tenant or class starvation. A protected Recovery Reserve keeps retention, emergency compaction, purge, repair, fencing, and safe shutdown viable. Healthy, soft-pressure, and hard-pressure Disk Pressure States drive explicit throttling and rejection; hard pressure rejects new ingestion and disk-growing maintenance without disabling safe reads or recovery paths. Every queue is bounded, overload produces typed retryable responses and observable reasons, quota changes are audited, and Kubernetes resource controls supplement rather than replace native admission.
