# Make live tail bounded, resumable, and explicitly at least once

Live tail emits only committed logs and atomically bridges an optional bounded historical query into commit following without a handoff gap. Opaque authenticated cursors carry per-shard commit positions and are bound to a tenant and filter; reconnection is at least once, cursor expiry is explicit, and retention or compaction may make old cursors unusable. Subscription memory, output rate, and idle lifetime are budgeted, and lagging consumers are disconnected with an explicit error and safe cursor rather than causing unbounded buffering or silent record loss. Tail headers identify whether ordering is live CommitVector or historical temporal ordering followed by live CommitVector. Planning and resumed physical work remain part of one durable lease usage aggregate, and any terminal that emits a cursor retains its lease so that cursor remains resumable until expiry.
