# Make governance auditing a durable fail-closed contract

Governance audit records live in a kernel-owned append-only store, separate from tenant telemetry, and security-relevant administrative mutations are acknowledged only when their catalog change and redacted audit record durably commit. Tenant purge cannot erase governance history, system policy controls its retention, and tenant administrators can read only their own subset while system administrators can read all records. Hash chaining plus periodic Instance Integrity Key signatures detects offline corruption and rollback against a trusted checkpoint without claiming protection from a compromised authorized process or independently exported old copy; if the audit store is unavailable, administrative mutations fail closed.
